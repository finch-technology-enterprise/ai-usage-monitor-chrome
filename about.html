AI Usage Quota Monitor

Quota usage for nine AI providers, right from your browser toolbar.

Open source · MIT License

About

AI Usage Monitor is a personal Manifest V3 Chrome extension that shows your subscription quota usage for OpenCode, Claude, ChatGPT / Codex, GitHub Copilot, Google Gemini, Cursor, Perplexity, Mistral Le Chat, and xAI Grok at a glance.

Enable only the providers you subscribe to in Settings — disabled providers are hidden. OpenCode is key-based: paste your Go plan API key in Settings. Every other provider uses your existing signed-in browser session, and none of those session cookies are copied or stored.

Usage data is fetched directly from each provider and never leaves your machine. No analytics, no tracking, no external servers — local-only.

Dock launcher (macOS)

The usage window can be launched from the Dock instead of the browser toolbar. From the extension folder, run:

bash install-launcher.sh

then drag AI Usage Monitor from ~/Applications into the Dock and choose Options → Keep in Dock. To remove it later:

bash uninstall-launcher.sh
